## 2. Test Walkthrough 1: Genuine Clean Credential (`SCENARIO-1-CLEAN`)

### Step 1: Login & Command Center
1. Navigate to `http://127.0.0.1:5173/login`.
2. Click the quick-fill button **Screening Officer** (`officer@verisight.gov.in`), click **Sign in to Terminal**.
3. Point out the live database metrics on the **Security Command Center** (Recharts volume trend, risk distribution, recent screenings).

### Step 2: Intake & Ingestion
1. Click **Initiate Screening** in the top bar or sidebar.
2. Target specification: **Passport (ICAO TD3)**, Issuing State: **India (IND)**.
3. Select Evaluation Scenario: **Scenario 1: Clean Genuine Passport**.
4. Drag and drop `demo_data/SCENARIO-1-CLEAN.jpg` into the upload zone (or click to browse).
5. Click **Commence Full Screening**. Notice the real-time progress steps:
   - *Hashing evidence with SHA-256...*
   - *Assessing optical sharpness and glare...*
   - *Rectifying orientation & Hough line deskewing...*
   - *Running OCR and computing ICAO 9303 check digits...*

### Step 3: Optical Quality Review
1. The app automatically redirects to `/screenings/{id}/quality`.
2. Highlight the metrics: Laplacian sharpness variance ($> 160$), glare ratio ($< 1\%$), estimated DPI ($300$).
3. Toggle between **Rectified / Preprocessed** and **Original Raw Scan**.
4. Click **Proceed to OCR Extraction**.

### Step 4: Visual Inspection Zone (VIZ) & MRZ Studio
1. Review the interactive bounding box overlay on the document canvas.
2. Click any field in the right-hand table (e.g. `SURNAME` or `PASSPORT NO`); observe the canvas immediately highlight and center the corresponding bounding box.
3. Click **Proceed to MRZ Studio & Cross-Check**.
4. Demonstrate the 7-3-1 mathematical check digits breakdown. All four check digits (Doc Number, DOB, Expiry, Composite) show green **VALID DIGIT** badges.
5. VIZ vs MRZ cross-check table shows green **MATCH** status across all fields.

### Step 5: Forensics & Multi-Factor Anomaly Diagnostics
1. Click **Proceed to Forensics Lab**.
2. Notice the side-by-side viewer: Original scan alongside the uniform ELA Error Heatmap.
3. All 5 pillars (ELA, Metadata, Texture, Alignment, Portrait) report **INFORMATIONAL: Baseline Consistent**.
4. Click **Proceed to Biometric Face Match (Optional)**.

### Step 6: Ephemeral Biometric Face Verification (DPDP Act 2023 Consent Gate)
1. The app navigates to `/screenings/{id}/face`.
2. Emphasize the **Statutory Biometric Consent Gate (DPDP Act 2023 §6)** banner. Biometric verification cannot be executed until subject affirmative consent is confirmed.
3. Check the consent box: *"Subject has provided affirmative consent for one-time ephemeral identity comparison."*
4. Inspect the **Extracted Document Portrait** automatically localized from the ICAO TD3 passport photo window.
5. In the **Live Subject Capture** card, either:
   - Click **Webcam** &rarr; **Capture Photo** (for live camera verification), or
   - Click **Select Live File** to upload a live comparison photo.
6. Click **Execute Biometric Match**:
   - The engine computes spatial gradient orientation histograms (HOG) and $\text{YCrCb}$ color descriptors in volatile RAM.
   - Shows **BIOMETRIC MATCH CONFIRMED** (Similarity score $> 75\%$, Confidence Band: `HIGH`, Optical Quality: `ACCEPTABLE`).
   - Explain to the jury: Live capture is never stored on disk or DB, honoring strict DPDP Act ephemeral memory guarantees.
7. Click **Continue to Risk Engine**.
8. Result: **CLEAR** (Composite Score: `0.0 / 100`).
