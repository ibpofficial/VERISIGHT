# VERISIGHT Explainable Risk Engine Specification
## Engine Version: `VERISIGHT-RULES-v1.2`
### SIH 2026 Problem Statement 26188 // Decision-Support Framework

---

## 1. Engine Philosophy & Non-Negotiable Axioms

1. **Deterministic, Not Black-Box**: The risk model is governed strictly by verifiable mathematical rules and weighted indicators. No opaque, unexplainable embeddings make autonomous clearance determinations.
2. **Decision-Support, Not Final Arbiter**: The system assists authorized officers. An officer or reviewer always makes the sovereign final call.
3. **Poor Quality $\neq$ Fraud**: An image with motion blur, lighting glare, or low resolution is flagged for a **Rescan** (`REVIEW`), never falsely accused of counterfeit fraud (`HIGH_RISK`).
4. **Anti-Correlation De-Duplication**: When optical quality is degraded, correlated noise variance and optical character confidence alarms are suppressed to avoid double-counting noise as fraud.
5. **Separation of Critical vs Weak Indicators**: Critical signals (such as mathematical ICAO check digit breakdown or Photoshop headers) instantly enforce a `HIGH_RISK` escalation regardless of overall score.

## 3. Mathematical Scoring Formulation

Let $T$ be the set of triggered rules. The composite risk score $S$ is computed as:
$$S = \min\left(100.0, \, \max\left(0.0, \, \sum_{r \in T} \text{weight}(r)\right)\right)$$

### Classification Mapping:
$$\text{RiskLevel} = \begin{cases} 
\text{HIGH\_RISK} & \text{if } \left(\exists r \in T : \text{is\_critical}(r) = \text{True}\right) \;\lor\; S \ge 65.0 \\
\text{REVIEW} & \text{if } \text{rescan\_recommended} = \text{True} \;\lor\; S \ge 35.0 \\
\text{CLEAR} & \text{otherwise } (S < 35.0)
\end{cases}$$

## 4. Supervisory Decision Override Protocol

1. **Authorized Roles**: `ADMINISTRATOR` and `SENIOR_REVIEWER` exclusively.
2. **Endpoint**: `POST /api/v1/risk/{id}/override`
3. **Mandatory Payload**: Target classification (`CLEAR`, `REVIEW`, `HIGH_RISK`) and detailed text justification ($\ge 10$ characters).
4. **Audit Trail**: The override does NOT erase the model's original determination. Both `risk_level` and `reviewer_override_level` are preserved side-by-side, and an immutable signed block is appended to the SHA-256 audit ledger.
